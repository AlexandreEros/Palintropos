Explorer

The analytical workspace: one selection, four coordinates. The globe below draws the same quantity, saved time and surface.

Give each coordinate a role. Two plotting axes give a field, one a line, none a single value. A preset only fills in the roles. Roles a coordinate does not accept are disabled, with the reason. Changing the quantity keeps the roles, values, statistics and interval. Click a field, the globe or a plot to move the linked cursor.

Horizontal wind

ψ contours are drawn at a uniform interval, so their spacing Δn ≈ Δψ/|v_rot|: closer lines mean faster rotational flow. Streamlines follow the full wind. Display settings send no request.
