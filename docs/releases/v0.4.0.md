# Palintropos v0.4.0: Operation Planet One (draft, not released)

**Status: draft for the owner's readiness review. Not tagged or published.**

v0.4.0 marks the anniversary of the owner's 2024 Mercury prediction (27
November). It adds:
- a general illumination geometry from one orbit and rotation;
- general stratified, nonlinear conduction in airless substrate columns;
- a verified Mercury experiment (VPW99 model TWO);
- portable C++ as the single canonical substrate step, GPU-first;
- one viewer for atmospheric and airless archives.

## Highlights

### Operation Planet One: Mercury

Evidence: `docs/validation/PLANET-ONE-GATES.md` (frozen before any results),
`docs/validation/PLANET-ONE-STATUS.md` and `docs/validation/planet-one-evidence/`.

- **Illumination geometry (#66).** One orbit and rotation definition drives
  the insolation of every experiment. Gate G-A passes.
- **Stratified nonlinear conduction (#67).** Gate G-B passes:
  - temperature-dependent conductivity and the Ledlow heat-capacity law;
  - a diagnosed massless (Kirchhoff) skin;
  - verified orders in space and time, energy closure, and a Jacobian
    checked against finite differences.
- **Mercury columns, VPW99 model TWO, G = 0.02 W m⁻².**
  - G-P passes, with owner-approved amendment A1 and estimator correction E1;
    the original criterion's failure is preserved.
  - G-I is satisfied by the four-level evidence (R2); the strict three-level
    failure (R1) stays on record.
  - G-M L3 was evaluated within the 10 K investigation threshold, with no
    parameter changed.
- **Global Mercury (G-G):** 2°, 16,200 columns, CuPy FP64 on a T4.
  - PERIODIC; the standalone replay of six columns is bitwise.
  - Global emitted − absorbed = 0.0202 W m⁻² = G.
- **Configuration-defined airless worlds (#72, #73):** saved configurations,
  finite transients, and a Ceres demonstration.

### The substrate, consolidated (#73–#83)

Evidence: `docs/validation/CONSOLIDATION-AUDIT.md` and
`docs/validation/consolidation-evidence/`.

- Canonical horizontal quadrature and vertical meshes live in `spatial`, and
  the time step in `temporal`; a `numerics` layer is added.
- Experiment-specific defaults are removed from the substrate and materials.
- Diagnostic point collections publish per-point diagnostics, never global
  means.
- Portable C++ is the canonical substrate step, executed GPU-first. The CPU
  path runs only on explicit request, with no fallback. The Python reference
  was removed after bitwise gates.
- On the T4: P2 met (21.0 ms per step); one host synchronization per step.

### Viewer 3 (#86)

Evidence: `docs/validation/viewer3/`.

- Held–Suarez, Mars with MOLA terrain, THAI, MOST and airless archives such
  as Mercury open in the same viewer. A quantity is explored by giving
  longitude, latitude, a vertical coordinate and time each a role.
- Viewer 2's maps, columns, sections and zonal means are reproduced exactly:
  742 matched comparisons, byte-identical frames.
- New vertical coordinate: geopotential height above the local surface,
  Z − Zₛ, for the atmosphere; signed depth and height for substrate columns
  (native levels only).
- New: time series and time × vertical plots.
- Airless archives are read by their file contract, with verified sample
  times and cadences and bounded memory.

## Known limitations and open items

- **P3:** at most 20 kernel launches per substrate step is **not met** (40).
  It is outstanding, documented, and neither satisfied nor weakened.
- **Mercury model idealizations:**
  - a point-source Sun, where VPW99 uses a finite disk;
  - fixed orbital elements, exact 3:2 rotation and zero obliquity;
  - uniform flat material, with no topography, shadowing or lateral
    conduction;
  - an ambiguous calorie convention (6.9 × 10⁻⁴ relative).
- **Mercury numerics:** sunrise backward-Euler time error of about 1 K at
  the production timestep (a stated limitation).
- **L4 observational comparison:** not evaluated.
- **THAI Ben 2 upper-level oscillation:** open (#47).
- **Viewer:**
  - no zonal means of columnar archives;
  - no reconstruction between native depths;
  - no mixed-domain surfaces;
  - no integrals or statistics;
  - geocentric coordinates and elapsed time only.
- **Atmospheric histories** synthesize one column per saved state (about
  0.4 s per state at T42 on the CPU).
- **Full T4 CUDA regression notebook:** not run for this release.
